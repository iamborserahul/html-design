ABOUT US

Built on Strength.
Driven by Quality.

Since 1998, Khodiyar Steel Industries has been manufacturing durable metal furniture and fabricated steel solutions for homes, hospitals, hotels, institutions, and commercial projects. With a strong focus on quality, functionality, and reliable production, we serve bulk buyers, distributors, OEM partners, and export markets from our manufacturing facility in Surat, Gujarat.

We specialize in metal furniture, storage solutions, and custom steel fabrication, combining practical design, durable materials, and efficient manufacturing to meet the needs of modern businesses and institutions.
