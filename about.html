COMPANY HISTORY

A Legacy of Pure
Fabrication Excellence

Founded in 1998 in Surat, Gujarat, Khodiyar Steel Industries has spent over 25 years establishing a reputation for uncompromising strength, precision-grade manufacturing, and high-end styling in structural metal fabrication.

What started as a specialized custom metalwork studio has grown into a state-of-the-art industrial manufacturing plant. Today, we cater to luxury household residences, large-scale commercial complexes, and critical institutional buyers like hospitals, hotels, and universities throughout India.

GUIDING VALUES

Mission, Vision & Journey

HISTORICAL MILESTONES

Our Entire Journey

1998

Foundation & Core Vision

Khodiyar Steel Industries is established in Surat, Gujarat with a humble local workshop and a strong promise: to fabricate high-durability, long-lasting metal products for everyday residential use.

2005

Industrial Infrastructure Expansion

Relocated to a highly spacious corporate industrial sector in Surat. Upgraded sheet slitting machinery, expanded welding grids, and began high-volume custom production for regional clients.

2012

Hospital & Institutional Division

Launched a highly specialized sterile clinical ward fabrication line. Began supplying heavy-duty Fowler beds, bedside lockers, and saline stands to hospitals, hotels, and universities throughout Gujarat.

2018

CNC Precision & Oven Powder Coating

Upgraded our facility with high-end CNC sheet metal bending lines, automated seam welding, and a state-of-the-art 7-tank chemical pre-treatment and thermal powder-coating oven grid.

2024

Home Edition Metallic Trends

Introduced the Home Edition catalog containing ultra-modern bunk beds, sliding luxury wardrobe almirahs, garden gazebos, and structural outdoor swings, distributing products to over 500+ national dealers.

COMMON INQUIRIES

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you support completely custom steel fabrication sizes?

Yes. We specialize in custom metal doors, structural safety gates, outdoor swings, and landscape gazebos fabricated to your exact dimensional layouts and structural specifications.

Are almirahs and cupboards rust-proofed?

Absolutely. Every cupboard, cabinet, and vanity frame undergoes strict rust-inhibiting phosphating and high-temperature oven-baked powder-coating finishes to survive humid Indian atmospheres.

Do you supply bulk institutional steel furniture for hospitals or colleges?

Yes, we serve bulk commercial distributors, OEM builders, hospital networks (for ICU/fowler beds), and large hostels with optimized factory prices and high-capacity manufacturing runs.
